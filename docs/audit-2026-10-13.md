# Project audit, 2026-10-13

Working document for the 2026-10-13 audit (first review since Opus 5.5).
Inputs: three read-only code audits (generator/pipeline, compose/quality/evaluate,
test and measurement infrastructure), a survey of `~/src/daydream` model work,
offline replays of cached generations, and two essays:
[agent-first repos](https://agent-hypervisor.ai/posts/agent-first-repos/) and
[graph engineering](https://agent-hypervisor.ai/posts/graph-engineering/).

Status: complete. Outcomes, prediction results and follow-ups are at the end.

## Headline findings

1. **The only gating metric no longer tracks human preference.** At N=34
   rated reviews, `height_outlier_score` has Spearman rho = -0.04 (p = 0.83).
   Held-out check: rho was +0.30 on the 16 reviews used to select it and
   -0.22 on the 18 reviews since. The selection bar (p < 0.3 across 16
   candidate metrics) expects about 5 false positives under the null, so this
   is a failed replication, not drift. `baseline_alignment` (+0.29 pooled) and
   `word_readability_rate` (+0.21) are the only consistent positives, both weak.
2. **The persistent "superscript" / `size_inconsistent` defect has mechanical
   causes in the compose layer**, not a perceptual one:
   - The baseline outlier clamp in `compose_words` compares raw row indices
     across word canvases with different heights. On seed 42 of the demo text
     it fires on 12/40 words with 0 true positives, moving words 5-9 px
     (10-18 px after 2x upscale) up or down. Words it raises match the
     reviewer's flags in review `2026-04-19_215858` (`exactly`, `maybe`,
     `something`).
   - `detect_baseline` returns the descender tip for narrow descender words
     (`by`) and for stitched contractions (`they'd`), raising the whole word
     by the descender depth.
   - `stitch_contraction` pads by the ink-bottom row index instead of the gap
     below the ink, doubling any bottom mismatch between halves, and aligns
     ink bottoms so a `y` descender in the left half pulls the right half down.
3. **The sizing stages fight each other and destroy short words.**
   `normalize_font_size` -> `equalize_body_zones` -> `harmonize_heights` ->
   `harmonize_heights_pass2` each resample the previous output. On seed 42,
   12/40 words (every short all-body word: `was`, `on`, `so`, `too`, `two`,
   `said`, `I`, `it`, `a`, `by`) are shrunk to ~0.2x by the body-zone pass,
   then re-inflated by the total-height pass to the same ~0.39x they had
   after normalization. Net effect of body-zone equalization: zero size change,
   plus blur. `by` passes through a 15 px tall bottleneck and arrives with no
   dark ink at all (darkest pixel 161).
4. **The earlier conclusion that x-height is the wrong lever was confounded.**
   `docs/sizing_diagnostic.md` attributed the superscript read to perception
   ("the eye compares top extents"). Both arms of that experiment carried the
   clamp bug and the resize chain, so the experiment could not separate
   lever from defect.
5. **The regression gate cannot see the defects humans flag.** It generates
   the five style-reference words (`Quick brown foxes jump high`, all 4-5
   chars) with 1 candidate, skips `equalize_body_zones` (production runs it),
   and has produced identical per-seed scores at every commit since `1fc02ad`.
6. **Human ground truth is not durable.** `reviews/human/*.json` is
   gitignored, `scripts/prune_reviews.py --apply` deletes reviews, 26 of 34
   composition ratings came from unseeded runs, and review JSONs do not record
   which image was rated.

## Pre-registered predictions (written before measurement)

P1, measured on cached seeds 42/137/2718 with generation held fixed:

- Single-resample sizing: final per-word ink heights unchanged within +/-1 px
  for >= 90% of words; short all-body words keep dark ink (darkest pixel
  < 60) where today several do not; `by` becomes legible.
- Clamp removal: words currently clamped move to their detected baselines;
  zero words remain more than 2 px from their detected baseline after
  placement (excluding jitter).
- Contraction stitch fix: right half of `they'd` sits on the left half's
  body baseline, not on the `y` descender tip.
- `height_outlier_score` on the composed demo text stays >= 0.90.
- Human composition rating: no prediction of magnitude; direction up, driven
  by the disappearance of "superscript" flags on descender words.

## Outcomes

### Prediction results

| Prediction | Result |
|---|---|
| Single resample keeps final ink heights within +/-1px for >= 90% of words | Falsified: 29/40 and 33/40. Short words land 2-4px taller; the old chain lost those pixels to truncation and threshold erosion at each resample. The policy's intended bounds are now hit. |
| Short all-body words keep dark ink after single resample | Wrong instrument: stroke-weight harmonization re-darkens everything. The real damage was blur, visible on contact sheets ("by" smudge to legible). |
| Zero words more than 2px off their detected baseline after clamp removal | Confirmed by eye on contact sheets of all 80 words (seeds 42, 137) after the detect_baseline fixes; before them, it, for, by and they'd were misdetected. |
| they'd right half on the body baseline | Confirmed. |
| height_outlier_score stays >= 0.90 on the composed demo text | Falsified under zone sizing (0.0-0.43): the metric rewards equal total height, which zone sizing gives up on purpose. Replaced as the gate metric. |
| Human prefers zone sizing on >= 2 of 3 seeds | Confirmed 3/3, blind, generation held fixed. Model judge also 3/3. |

### What changed (commits on main)

1. Contraction stitch aligns halves on detected baselines.
2. Sizing: one resample per word (`plan_word_scales`, `size_words`); `pipeline.postprocess_words` is the single stage graph.
3. Compose: no row-index clamp; word-aware `detect_baseline`; first-line words no longer dropped.
4. Candidate-score logging crash fixed.
5. Zone sizing policy, then made default after the human A/B.
6. Seeded runs (`--seed`), run manifest JSON, demo.sh and archive metrics from the manifest, GPU numerics scoped to `run()`, review JSONs record rated images.
7. Metric-correlation report with held-out replication; prune never deletes.
8. Gate: `x_height_consistency`, production topology, short words, strict primary metrics, OCR floor on 3+ letter words, deterministic; re-baselined with approval.
9. Review JSONs tracked.
10. Punctuated words recurse through `generate_word`; all trailing marks synthesized; marks sized by `estimate_x_height`.
11. CLAUDE.md restructured (707 -> 332 lines); docs moved and corrected; README corrected.

### Lessons applied from the two essays

- Agent-first repos: CLAUDE.md as rules plus pointers; predictions written before experiments (`docs/experiment-log.jsonl`); fresh-context review (blind model judge); the evaluator treated as the human's (approval required for gate changes, written into CLAUDE.md); ground truth kept append-only and versioned; "let it see what it built" (contact sheets found the bugs metrics hid).
- Graph engineering: the sizing/compose topology was implicit and duplicated (8 hand-wired copies, one of which, the gate, skipped a production stage); it is now one function. Runs are captured (seed + manifest) and downstream stages replayed offline with one variable changed. Instruments were verified before trusting changes (two metrics measure something other than their names; the gate metric failed held-out replication). Compute could be routed to uncertainty next: see follow-ups.

### Notes on ~/src/daydream and local models

daydream runs a production vLLM server (`cyankiwi/Qwen3.5-9B-AWQ-4bit`, ~9 GB, `--language-model-only`) and never evaluated vision models. The cached checkpoint includes a vision tower, so a local VLM judge or second OCR reader is possible without a download, but it would need to run when daydream's GPU slice allows (its arbiter lock is at `/srv/daydream/data/gpu.lock`). A VLM used as an OCR reader should not be told the target word (language-model "correction" inflates readability). Not adopted here: the Claude judge needs no infrastructure and agreed with the human 3/3.

## Follow-ups (not done)

Ordered by expected value.

1. **Composition review under the new pipeline** (`make test-human EVAL=composition,sizing,baseline`): the test of the 4/5 target and of the In Progress findings.
2. **Word spacing collapses on some pairs** ("rememberexactly", "brownfoxes", "soonby"): `WORD_SPACING=6` with +/-4px jitter can leave 2px; the minimum gap should scale with x-height.
3. **Candidate eval scorer mismatch**: the candidate eval ranks with `quality_score_breakdown` defaults, not production's scorer (stroke reference, word length, 40% OCR blend, style tiebreak). Fix before reweighting `QUALITY_WEIGHTS`.
4. **Comma reads as period at composition scale** (Caveat comma tail too short); `?!` after long words small.
5. **Adaptive compute** (graph engineering: spend where uncertain): best-of-N is a flat 3 per word; spend more candidates on short and punctuated words, which fail OCR most, and fewer on long common words.
6. **Model-judge calibration**: run the blind judge on every future A/B and log agreement; promote it to a pre-push advisory only after agreement holds over ~10 sessions.
7. **Broken diagnostics**: `check_baseline_alignment` measures canvas bottoms; `_xheight_ratio` is ~1.0 for all words. Fix or remove.
8. **Compute-rule cleanups** in `ddim_sample` (uncond context moved per call, `zero_style` and `DDIMScheduler` rebuilt per call); `_get_ocr_fn` ImportError guard cannot fire.
9. **Edge inputs**: `--candidates 0` and `--steps 0` crash; standalone "." or "?" tokens score an empty OCR target; very wide words can clip at the page edge on narrow pages.
10. **ab_harness** ignores the `scheduler` and `postprocess` experiment settings (both compare identical configs).
11. **TESTING.md** predates these changes (counts, gate description); refresh with its skill.
12. **x_height_consistency moves in whole-pixel steps** (code review WARN): it compares medians of integer pixel measurements over 3 words per group, so a 1px change in either median moves the score by 0.05-0.07, at the gate's tolerance. The gate is deterministic, so this is sensitivity rather than flakiness. Switching to means (sub-pixel) needs a re-baseline, which is the user's call.
13. **Remaining code review NOTEs** (CODEREVIEW.md): `_reinforce_thin_strokes` covers single-character words only although zone sizing shrinks short words to ~0.23x; all-mark tokens like "..." still go to DiffusionPen and likely render blank; `config.py` (PRIMARY_METRICS, ZONE_*) is not covered by the `.claude/settings.json` ask rules; the push gate passes when CUDA is hidden.

## Process note

The code review caught a defect this audit introduced: a scripted README edit called `str.replace("", text)` (an empty search string from a bad slice) and grew README.md from 17 KB to 14 MB. The check after the edit grepped for stale phrases, which cannot detect damage elsewhere. The fix was folded into the original commit before pushing, so the blob never reached GitHub. Rule: after any scripted edit, look at `git diff --stat` and the diff itself, and assert search strings are non-empty.
